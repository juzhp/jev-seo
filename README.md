## What you get

- **One ordered fix list** — not 300 warnings. Fixes ranked by impact and effort; quick wins separated from long-term projects.
- **9 area scores + overall score** — weak spots visible at a glance.
- **AI page review** — every important page read and scored for helpfulness, specificity, trust, and AI-search citability.
- **Rankings, keywords, backlinks** — which keywords are one push from page one, and how your links compare with competing sites.
- **AI crawler checks** — can GPTBot, PerplexityBot and Google-Extended reach your site? 12 crawlers checked.
- **Real-user speed** — Core Web Vitals from real Chrome users, not just lab scores.
- **PDF, Excel, Markdown** — reports ready to send to clients.

## How it works

1. Crawls the whole site: status codes, redirects, sitemap, robots, canonicals.
2. Checks everything against Google's official guidelines.
3. AI reads each important page; low-confidence judgments are flagged for human review, never forced.
4. Pulls rankings, keywords and backlinks, compared with competing sites.
5. Scores, ranks, and writes a plain-language executive summary.

Numbers are computed by code. AI interprets content — it never invents data, and missing data is labeled missing.
